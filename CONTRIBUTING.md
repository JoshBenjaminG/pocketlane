# Contributing

Contributions to Pocketlane are welcome when they concern the emulator interface, accessibility, performance, tests, documentation, or original assets.

Do not submit:

- Commercial or otherwise unauthorized ROMs
- BIOS or firmware dumps
- Game artwork, screenshots, music, or other copyrighted assets without redistribution permission
- Decryption keys or instructions primarily intended to bypass access controls
- Links to unauthorized game downloads

Test changes with legally obtained personal dumps, original homebrew, or freely redistributable test ROMs. Do not commit those files unless their license explicitly permits redistribution and the project has approved their inclusion.
